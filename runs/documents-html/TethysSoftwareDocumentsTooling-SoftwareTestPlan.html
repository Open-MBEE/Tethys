Software requirements without a verification case
shortName name documentation
TF-010 processorMemoryReserve The flight software's processor load and memory footprint shall close against the flight computer with the reserve the active software margin policy row (TethysMarginPolicy::activeSoftwarePolicy) prescribes. Rationale: the reserve is the policy's, not this row's, so a policy change re-evaluates every budget at once (TS-142).
TF-011 busReserve The AABP-1 bus schedule of the full modes (proximity operations, capture, despin) shall close against the frame with the reserve the active software margin policy row prescribes. Rationale: the bus is the one shared resource every application uses; the schedule currently uses more slots than the reserve allows, so this row is open and its satisfaction fails until the maintainer resizes the schedule or the policy (TS-143).
TF-012 busReserveCoast The AABP-1 bus schedule of the coast mode shall close against the frame with the reserve the active software margin policy row prescribes. Rationale: coast is the mode the tug spends most of the mission in, so its reserve is the one the growth of the mission actually consumes (TS-143).
TF-023 busMessaging The AABP-1 software bus shall provide a topic for every software component, a receive queue of at least one message per topic and a maximum message no longer than the largest command packet. Rationale: a component without a topic cannot publish, and a message longer than a packet cannot be relayed to the ground (TS-143).
