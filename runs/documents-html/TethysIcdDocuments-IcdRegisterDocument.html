Tethys Retrieval interface control document register

One interface control document per TethysInterfaces interface definition, each specialising its interface def with the parameters both sides must agree on, bound to both ends, under the control authority its InterfaceControl metadata names; the software ICDs (TI-02x) of TethysSoftwareIcds profile the packet and data-bus interfaces down to message, frame and register level.

ICD classes

Interface control documents of the mission
name documentation
PacketInterface The command/telemetry packet interface between the flight software and the operations centre, independent of the RF link that carries it.
TugInspectorIcd Argent ↔ Wren while stowed: 28 V keep-alive power, checkout telemetry over the umbilical and the separation signal. The mechanical and thermal disciplines of the stowed stack are TI-007 and TI-008 over their own backbone interfaces.
LaunchIcd Stack ↔ Arcturus-5H: adapter geometry, quasi-static and dynamic loads, fairing environments, stack mass properties, separation and injection accuracy.
SpaceGroundIcd Argent ↔ Meridian: X-band up and down, Ka-band down, modulation, coding, rate envelope, two-way ranging and the packet protocol over the link. The Earth-range envelope per phase is in TethysLinkBudgets.
InspectorRelayIcd Wren ↔ Argent proximity link: UHF, low rate down to Wren, imagery and radar up to Argent, usable within the geometry window below; the data volume it must carry depends on the target size and is sized in TethysLinkBudgets.
CaptureIcd Argent ↔ Amaranth through capture: contact loads, grapple torque, the spin the capture system absorbs and the contact thermal environment. The spin and load figures are the large-target case; the small and nominal cases follow from the target cases in TethysLinkBudgets and the capture analyses.
DockingIcd Argent ↔ visiting vehicle in the DRO: a passive androgynous ring on the tug, power transfer and a data link across the mated interface.
PowerIcd The regulated 28 V bus from the EPS to every subsystem, with its ripple and the current limit each load may draw.
HighVoltagePowerIcd The unregulated high-voltage bus from the arrays to the SEP power processing.
DataBusIcd The on-board data bus: a dual-redundant serial bus at 1 Mbit/s carrying packets between the bus controller and every remote terminal.
ThermalIcd A conductive thermal interface: bolted joint conductance and the heat it carries at the interface temperature band.
InspectorDispenserIcd Argent ↔ Wren mechanical: the dispenser rails, preload, release spring and the ejection velocity; connected over the structure ports the backbone exposes.
InspectorThermalIcd Argent ↔ Wren thermal while stowed: the dispenser keeps Wren's battery above its survival temperature with heat conducted through the rails.
PropellantFeedIcd Tank ↔ ground fill-and-drain for xenon and hydrazine: fill pressure, flow rate and the mass loaded.
FanoutTopic One message type from its publisher to two subscribers: a three-ended interface, which the standard permits for any interface not typed by BinaryInterface.
BroadcastTopic One message type from its publisher to three subscribers: a four-ended interface.
OnBoardBusProtocolIcd The AABP-1 protocol between the software bus controller in the board support package and each remote terminal on the on-board data bus: frame layout, addressing, the time-slot schedule within one polling period and the handling of check failures. The bus electrical and rate figures are those of TI-013.
SpaceLinkProfileIcd The MLS-2 profile of the TI-010 packet interface between the telecom application and the Meridian packet gateway: CCSDS Space Packets with a time-code secondary header, carried in fixed-length transfer frames on eight virtual channels, with a rate set per tug mode and a safe rate set that every mode falls back to. The RF carrier beneath it is TI-003.
InspectorRelaySoftwareIcd The software profile of the proximity relay between the inspector-handling application and Wren: AABP-1 frames tunnelled over the TI-004 GMSK link, the identifier block the relayed inspector packets use, the store-and-forward depth on the tug and the acknowledgement rules.
RemoteInterfaceUnitRegisterIcd The register-level ICD between the remote-interface-unit driver of the board support package and each remote interface unit: the register access frame AABP-1 carries, the address and data widths, and the two register maps.

Interface requirements

Requirements that both sides conform to each ICD
name documentation
tugInspectorConformance Argent and Wren shall conform to ICD-001 across the umbilical: keep-alive voltage 28 V, power 15 W, checkout telemetry at 9 600 bit/s.
launchConformance The stack shall conform to ICD-002: mass, centre of mass, loads, envelope and separation.
spaceGroundConformance Argent and Meridian shall conform to ICD-003: frequencies, modulation, coding, rates and ranging.
relayConformance Wren and Argent shall conform to ICD-004 across the proximity link within the geometry window.
captureConformance The capture system shall meet ICD-005 for every target case.
dockingConformance The docking ring shall meet ICD-006.
dispenserConformance The dispenser and Wren shall meet ICD-007.
inspectorThermalConformance The dispenser shall keep Wren above its survival temperature per ICD-008.
propellantFeedConformance Both fill-and-drain interfaces shall meet ICD-009.
packetConformance Flight software and the operations centre shall exchange only packets the dictionary defines, per TCD-010.
internalBusConformance Every subsystem shall meet ICD-011 to ICD-014 on its power, data and thermal interfaces.
busProtocolConformance The board support package and every remote terminal shall conform to SCD-020: AABP-1 frames of at most 1 024 B with an 8 B header and a 16-bit checksum, 32 addresses, 64 schedule slots per 125 ms polling period, two retries before a fault is raised.
spaceLinkProfileConformance The telecom application and the Meridian packet gateway shall conform to SCD-021: CCSDS Space Packets with a 10 B time-code secondary header in 1 115 B transfer frames on eight virtual channels, the rate set of the current tug mode, and the 2 000 bit/s safe rate set in safe and abort-hold modes.
relayProfileConformance The inspector-handling application and Wren shall conform to SCD-022: AABP-1 frames tunnelled over the proximity link, identifiers 128–159, acknowledgement within 2 s, three retries, 200 MB store-and-forward on the tug.
registerMapConformance The remote-interface-unit driver and both remote interface units shall conform to SCD-023: 8-bit register addresses, 16-bit data words, the register maps of the thermal and propulsion unit and of the mechanism unit.

busProtocolTest Bus protocol test on the avionics test bench with the flight bus controller and both remote interface units.

relayProfileTest Relay profile test with flight Wren on the proximity link in the anechoic chamber, including a store-and-forward backlog.
registerMapTest Register read-back test of every mapped register on both remote interface units.
