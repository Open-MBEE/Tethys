Tethys Retrieval interface control document register

One interface control document per TethysInterfaces interface definition, each specialising its interface def with the parameters both sides must agree on, bound to both ends, under the control authority its InterfaceControl metadata names.

ICD classes

Interface control documents of the mission
name documentation
PacketInterface The command/telemetry packet interface between the flight software and the operations centre, independent of the RF link that carries it.
TugInspectorIcd Argent ↔ Wren while stowed: 28 V keep-alive power, checkout telemetry over the umbilical and the separation signal. Mechanical and thermal disciplines are in TI-007 and TI-008 because the backbone interface has only umbilical ends.
LaunchIcd Stack ↔ Arcturus-5H: adapter geometry, quasi-static and dynamic loads, fairing environments, stack mass properties, separation and injection accuracy.
SpaceGroundIcd Argent ↔ Meridian: X-band up and down, Ka-band down, modulation, coding, rate envelope, two-way ranging and the packet protocol over the link. The Earth-range envelope per phase is in TethysLinkBudgets.
InspectorRelayIcd Wren ↔ Argent proximity link: UHF, low rate down to Wren, imagery and radar up to Argent, usable within the geometry window below; the data volume it must carry depends on the target size and is sized in TethysLinkBudgets.
CaptureIcd Argent ↔ Amaranth through capture: contact loads, grapple torque, the spin the capture system absorbs and the contact thermal environment. The spin and load figures are the large-target case; the small and nominal cases follow from the target cases in TethysLinkBudgets and the capture analyses.
DockingIcd Argent ↔ visiting vehicle in the DRO: a passive androgynous ring on the tug, power transfer and a data link across the mated interface.
PowerIcd The regulated 28 V bus from the EPS to every subsystem, with its ripple and the current limit each load may draw.
HighVoltagePowerIcd The unregulated high-voltage bus from the arrays to the SEP power processing.
DataBusIcd The on-board data bus: a dual-redundant serial bus at 1 Mbit/s carrying packets between the bus controller and every remote terminal.
ThermalIcd A conductive thermal interface: bolted joint conductance and the heat it carries at the interface temperature band.
InspectorDispenserIcd Argent ↔ Wren mechanical: the dispenser rails, preload, release spring and the ejection velocity; connected over the structure ports the backbone exposes.
InspectorThermalIcd Argent ↔ Wren thermal while stowed: the dispenser keeps Wren's battery above its survival temperature with heat conducted through the rails.
PropellantFeedIcd Tank ↔ ground fill-and-drain for xenon and hydrazine: fill pressure, flow rate and the mass loaded.

Interface requirements

Requirements that both sides conform to each ICD
name documentation
tugInspectorConformance Argent and Wren shall conform to ICD-001 across the umbilical: keep-alive voltage 28 V, power 15 W, checkout telemetry at 9 600 bit/s.
launchConformance The stack shall conform to ICD-002: mass, centre of mass, loads, envelope and separation.
spaceGroundConformance Argent and Meridian shall conform to ICD-003: frequencies, modulation, coding, rates and ranging.
relayConformance Wren and Argent shall conform to ICD-004 across the proximity link within the geometry window.
captureConformance The capture system shall meet ICD-005 for every target case.
dockingConformance The docking ring shall meet ICD-006.
dispenserConformance The dispenser and Wren shall meet ICD-007.
inspectorThermalConformance The dispenser shall keep Wren above its survival temperature per ICD-008.
propellantFeedConformance Both fill-and-drain interfaces shall meet ICD-009.
packetConformance Flight software and the operations centre shall exchange only packets the dictionary defines, per TCD-010.
internalBusConformance Every subsystem shall meet ICD-011 to ICD-014 on its power, data and thermal interfaces.
