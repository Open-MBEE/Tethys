L1 requirements
Id Name Statement Tier
TR-001 retrieval The mission shall deliver the target body 2031 QX, intact and with a mass of at least 100 t, into the designated lunar distant retrograde orbit. mission
TR-002 missionDuration The mission shall complete the insertion of the target into the distant retrograde orbit within 1 300 days of launch. mission
TR-003 characterisation Before committing to capture, the mission shall determine the target's shape, mass to within 10 % and spin state, and shall confirm that each lies within the capture system's design envelope. mission
TR-004 launchCompatibility The flight system shall be compatible with the Arcturus-5H launch vehicle and its direct injection to an Earth-escape trajectory. mission
TR-005 groundCompatibility The flight system shall communicate through the Meridian deep-space network at every Earth range of the mission. mission
TR-006 cislunarSafety The target body shall be placed in an orbit that remains bound to the Moon for at least 100 years without station-keeping, and no credible failure during the return shall place it on an Earth-impact trajectory. mission
TR-007 missionClass The flight system shall carry no single-point failure that ends the mission. mission
TR-008 deflectionDemonstration While attached to the target, the mission shall demonstrate a slow-push deflection and shall measure the resulting change in the target's heliocentric velocity to an accuracy of 1 mm/s. mission
TR-009 utilisationAccess After delivery, the tug shall hold the target in an attitude and with a docking interface that lets the visiting vehicle Lantern dock and reach the target's surface. mission
