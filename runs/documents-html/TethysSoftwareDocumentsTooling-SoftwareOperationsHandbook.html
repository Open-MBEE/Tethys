Argent flight software and Meridian Operations Suite operations handbook

How the flight software is operated through the Meridian Operations Suite, as TethysModes, TethysSequence, TethysFdir, TethysOperations and TethysGroundSoftware define it.

Flight modes

Flight modes
name documentation
launch From lift-off to the end of early operations: separation, array deployment, sun acquisition.
freeFlight Modes before capture. Entered at coast; re-entered at coast after a safe-mode recovery.
stacked Modes with the target captured: despin, return thrust and coast, DRO hold, visiting-vehicle docking, disposal. Re-entered at stackCoast after a safe-mode recovery.
safe Sun-pointed, low-gain, thrusters and high-voltage bus off, waiting for ground recovery; while in safe mode the sun-pointing hold is re-acquired every hour (the `do` behaviour).
abortHold Return aborted by the Earth-impact exclusion monitor: thrust inhibited, attitude held, high-gain link up, waiting for a replanned trajectory.

On-board sequences

Sequences by number
name sequenceId timeTagged documentation
OnBoardSequence 0 true A sequence the autonomy application can load and dispatch: a numbered, stored behaviour whose steps are time-tagged relative to its start. Every sequence below specialises it; TethysFlightBehaviour's sequencing engine loads them by number.
ThrustArc 1 true One thrust arc: the SEP string thrusts for the arc's duration at the given thrust and burns the xenon that duration implies; the result is the xenon left.
DespinSequence 2 true Despin the captured stack with the hydrazine thrusters: each pulse train lengthens the spin period by a fixed factor until the period is above the threshold at which the wheels take over.
InspectorReleaseSequence 3 true The inspector release as a timed go/no-go sequence: checkout, umbilical disconnect, a hold for the ground go, then release — each step gated by the state the previous one must have reached.
DroInsertionSequence 4 true The insertion into the distant retrograde orbit after the lunar assist: the post-assist trajectory is checked against the insertion corridor, the insertion arc is executed, and a miss leaves the stack in the backup heliocentric loiter orbit rather than in cislunar space. The arc length is an owned attribute, not a parameter: see docs/findings/WP-E.md F-E02.
DroStationKeeping 5 true The DRO checkout's station-keeping demonstration: hydrazine trims are repeated until the along-track drift is inside the control box, then the three demonstration cycles of the checkout plan are costed.
MissionSequence 6 true The flight from lift-off to disposal as an executable action over the phases of TethysMission::flightTimeline. The clock runs in days; the thrust arcs are loops counted against the arc plan; the capture commit is a decision over the delivered target estimate and the capture envelope, and a refused commit ends the mission in a retreat hold (`terminate`). The lunar assist is waited for as a timed event (`accept at` a clock instant scheduled after the return arcs).

Fault protection

Fault and on-board events
name documentation
FaultEvent A detected fault, raised by a monitor with the level it was detected at.
ThrusterFlameout A thruster's discharge has extinguished (domain failure modes F1, F3).
PpuTrip A power processing unit has tripped on over-current (F2, F7).
AttitudeLoss Attitude knowledge lost or rates above the control authority (F24).
BatteryLow Battery state of charge below the load-shed threshold (F25).
ProcessorReset The flight computer has rebooted (F27); the supervisor restarts its timers.
GroundContact Any valid telecommand received: the command-loss timer is re-armed.
TrajectoryUpdate A return-trajectory solution from navigation: the predicted Earth closest approach and the impact probability over the knowledge uncertainty.
InhibitChain The cinch inhibit chain as an object the checker runs with every input free.
FdirTug The tug with its FDIR supervisor beside the mode machine.
FaultInjector A test conductor injecting faults and events into the tug's command port.
CommandLossInjector Brings the tug to coast, keeps contact with passes on days 5 and 10, then falls silent: the timer expires on day 17, the tug safes, and a contact on day 23 followed by SafeModeExit on day 24 recovers it to coast.
ThrusterFaultInjector During the first thrust arc: two flameouts are restarted, the third switches to the spare, the fourth loses the string and safes the tug.
EarthImpactInjector A stacked return on a compressed clock (a ground pass every five days): the capture is completed by injected events, the return arc starts, a nominal trajectory update is accepted, then a solution inside the exclusion sphere aborts the return.
FaultScenario One tug under one fault injector.
Supervisor monitors
name type documentation
commandLossTimer Re-armed by every ground contact (the telecom subsystem reports one per pass); on expiry the supervisor calls safe mode (sun-pointed, low-gain omni), where the ground can reach the tug again.
thrusterHealth Flameout recovery: restart the thruster up to the retry limit, then switch to the spare; with the spare used and another string lost, the operating set cannot be met and the supervisor calls safe mode.
ppuHealth PPU trip recovery: cross-strap the thruster to the redundant PPU on the first trip; a second trip leaves the string on the remaining PPU and calls safe mode for the ground to replan the thrust profile.
powerHealth Battery monitor: below the load-shed fraction the supervisor sheds loads through safe mode; the ground clears it with SafeModeExit.
attitudeHealth Attitude monitor: loss of attitude knowledge or control goes straight to safe mode's sun acquisition on thrusters.
earthImpactExclusion The Earth-impact exclusion monitor: every trajectory update is checked against the exclusion sphere and the impact-probability limit; a violation aborts the return (AbortReturn → TugModes abortHold, thrust inhibited).

Flight rules and procedures

TG-010
The dictionary shall assign distinct application ids within the 11-bit range, distinct opcodes, mnemonics and event ids; every command shall have an opcode and a mode precondition, every packet shall sum to its length, every telemetry point shall name its source application and carry ordered limits, and every fault-supervisor threshold shall read a limit-table row.
FR-001
The flight system shall not thrust while the proximity link is active.
FR-002
The tug shall remain outside the 500 m keep-out sphere until a capture sequence is loaded and authorised.
FR-003
The imager boresight shall stay more than 25° from the Sun while its cover is open.
FR-004
Battery state of charge shall not fall below 40 % in any planned activity.
TG-020
Operations shall be declared ready only with three stations committed, every procedure validated on the flatsat, at least two end-to-end rehearsals flown, every contingency procedure rehearsed and every position staffed.
The presented status meets every criterion.
TG-021
Every trended resource shall project above its floor at the end of the nominal mission.
Xenon projects above its reserve.
Battery capacity projects above its floor.
TG-022
An extended mission shall be proposed only if the xenon left covers its station keeping with the reserve intact.
The two-year proposal fits the xenon left.
