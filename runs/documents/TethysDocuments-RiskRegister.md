# Tethys Retrieval — Risk Register

Risks are elements of the model annotated with RiskMetadata::Risk; this register is their current list.

## Open risks

*Elements annotated as risks*

| name | documentation |
| --- | --- |
| earthImpactRisk | The returned body could be placed on an Earth-impact trajectory. |
| targetSizeRisk | The target may be larger, heavier or faster-spinning than the characterisation<br>envelope (the design driver of the mission). |
| thrusterLifeRisk | The Hall thrusters may not reach the 20 000 h per-string life the return needs. |
| proximityOperationsRisk | Relative navigation at the target may be less robust than designed. |
| xenonMarginRisk | The xenon margin may be consumed by target mass growth. |
| captureBagTechnology | The inflatable capture bag is the mission's least mature technology. |
