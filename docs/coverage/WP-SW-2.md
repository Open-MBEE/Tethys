# WP-SW-2 coverage — flight dictionaries, packets and tables

Files: `model/64-flight-dictionary.sysml` (`TethysFlightDictionary`, mission tier, generated by
`scripts/gen-flight-dictionary.py` — the generator is deterministic and `--check` is a run);
`model/64-flight-dictionary-tooling.sysml` (`TethysFlightDictionaryDocuments`, tool tier); backbone
edits in `model/51-icds.sysml` (`PacketSecondaryHeader`, `SpacePacket` header lengths),
`model/53-operations.sysml` (`ApidTable` defaults, `Dictionary` counts and flags, `TG-010`) and

`5cd37f288` (`runs/tool-version.txt`); every command below is a `run` line in `scripts/runs.sh` and its
output is in `runs/<name>.txt`. Findings and probes in `docs/findings/WP-SW-2.md`.

(961 fields), 12 parameter tables with 48 rows, one six-row limit table; 12 applications. Every
consistency flag of `flightDictionary` evaluates `true` and `-satisfy`
shows `TG-010` (`requirements-satisfy`: `satisfy dictionaryConsistent by flightDictionary holds`).
