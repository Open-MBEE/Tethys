| F-SW1204 | WP-SW-12 | generator design | Generated tables are `static const` in every translation unit that includes a header | A pointer into `argent_packets[]` taken in one unit is not comparable with, or subtractable from, the array in another unit (UBSan/ASan reported a stack-buffer overflow when a service indexed by `packet - argent_packets` on a caller's pointer). | — | Flight code resolves indices by id/mnemonic across units (`packet_index()`), tests compare ids and names, never addresses.
