Design consequences in `model/69-ground-software.sysml`: the command builder's planted range
cases use the `Real` and `Natural` arguments of the dictionary (F-SW701); the pass planner's
volume calc is numeric (F-SW702); the sequencer's rule check is a calc read by the decision, not a
step in the succession (F-SW703); the requirements read Boolean attributes of their subjects, not calc results (F-SW704). The ground-to-flight exchange follows WP-SW-3's working forms:
the checker sends `LoadSequence` from its own port inside its performed action (no state-definition
port sends, F-S01), the flight stand-in receives through one inline exhibited state on one port
(F-S03), and the interface is the TI-021 `SpaceLinkProfileIcd` connected end to end (no binds,
F-S05). The flight rules are applied to the scalar fields of `PlannedSequence` bound one by one
into the calc's parameters, the form WP-SW-3's `CheckConstraints` uses.
